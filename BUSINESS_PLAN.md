## 1. Market Overview

### Polish Tutoring Market (2025-2026)
- Market size: **1B+ PLN/year**, growing 10-15% annually
- 70% of Polish parents use supplementary tutoring
- Average monthly household spend on tutoring: **896 PLN**
- 40% of tutoring is now fully online (post-pandemic shift)

### Math Tutoring Pricing
| Level | Rate (PLN/hr) |
|-------|--------------|
| Szkola podstawowa (kl. 2-8) | 60-100 |
| Liceum / Matura podstawowa | 70-120 |
| Matura rozszerzona | 90-150 |
| Online average | 64.82 |
| In-person average | 67.87 |

### Key Competitors
- **e-korepetycje.net** — largest platform, 370K monthly visits
- **Superprof.pl** — 134K monthly visits, international
- **BUKI (buki.org.pl)** — 91K monthly visits
- **ekorki.pl** — 77.6K monthly visits
- **Matplaneta** — premium brand, Harvard-based Math Circle methodology

### How Parents Find Tutors
1. Word of mouth / recommendations (#1 method)
2. Online platforms (e-korepetycje, Superprof)
3. Facebook parent groups
4. Google search
5. School community boards

### Seasonality
- **September-November**: highest demand (school year start)
- **January**: second semester peak
- **March-April**: pre-exam crunch (egzamin osmoklasisty May 12, matura May 5-24)
- **June-August**: low season (summer break)

## 4. Marketing Plan

### Phase 1: Quick Wins (Week 1-2, Budget: 20 PLN)

**Facebook (90% of marketing energy):**
- Create business profile
- Join 10+ parent groups (local + "Egzamin osmoklasisty 2026")
- Post template content: exam tips, common mistakes, success stories
- 3 posts/week on own page

**Direct outreach:**
- Message all friends/family about referrals
- Print flyers (A4 quartered) for schools, libraries, community centers

**Google My Business:**
- Set up profile for local visibility
- Category: Private tutor

### Phase 2: Content & Community (Weeks 3-8)

**Facebook content calendar:**
| Day | Content Type | Example |
|-----|-------------|---------|
| Monday | Zadanie tygodnia | Past exam problem, answer next day |
| Wednesday | Educational tip | "3 mistakes that cost points on egzamin" |
| Friday | Success story | Anonymous student progress story |

**Blog on website (2 articles/month):**
1. "Egzamin osmoklasisty 2026 — kompletny przewodnik dla rodzicow"
2. "5 sygnalow, ze Twoje dziecko potrzebuje korepetycji"
3. "Matura 2026 z matematyki — jak przygotowac sie w 3 miesiace"

**Partnerships:**
- Schools: propose free parent webinars on exam prep
- Libraries/community centers: free "Matematyka bez stresu" workshops
- Other tutors (non-math): mutual referral network

### Phase 3: Pre-Exam Crunch (March-April)

- Launch "Maraton przed egzaminem" package
- Optional Google Ads: 30 PLN/day targeting "egzamin osmoklasisty korepetycje [city]"
- Daily countdown posts on social media
- Post-exam follow-up for testimonials and summer course referrals

### Phase 4: Instagram (Month 2+, 10% energy)

- 2-3 posts/week + daily Stories
- Reels: quick math tricks (15-60 sec)
- Carousels: "5 wzorow na mature rozszerzona"
- Behind-the-scenes lesson content

### Word of Mouth Acceleration

**Referral program:**
- Existing client: -50 PLN off next package
- New client: first full lesson free
- Trigger: ask after 3-4 successful lessons

**Post-lesson parent updates:**
- Short email after each lesson (what was covered, progress, next steps)
- Pre-exam: free cheat sheet PDF with key formulas
- Post-exam: congratulations + feedback request

## 10. Success Principles

1. **Consistency > Intensity** — 3 posts/week for 3 months beats 10 posts for 1 week
2. **Value before sales** — 80% educational content, 20% promotion
3. **Local > National** — be #1 in your city before competing nationwide
4. **Testimonials = gold** — ask after every successful collaboration
5. **Deadline = leverage** — every March/April post should mention "zostalo X tygodni do egzaminu"
6. **Transparency builds trust** — show pricing, show your face, be authentic
7. **Parents decide, students stay** — marketing targets parents, teaching retains students
